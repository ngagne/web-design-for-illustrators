# Mission: Design for screens from an artist’s eye

## Why
Build a practical bridge from hand-drawn and painted work to graphic and web design, so visual instincts can become clear, usable digital work rather than being left behind at the canvas edge.

## Success looks like
- Turn a rough visual idea into a balanced, readable poster, page, or interface composition.
- Make intentional decisions about whitespace, type, colour, hierarchy, and responsive layout.
- Complete and explain a small portfolio-ready web or graphic design project.

## Constraints
- Starts from traditional-art experience; assumes no design-software or coding background.
- Lessons stay brief, visual, and practice-led.

## Out of scope
- Advanced front-end engineering, branding strategy, and professional print production for now.
