# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary learners are traditional sketching and painting artists working independently in a self-guided setting. They bring visual instincts from drawing, painting, character sketching, or digital art, but may have no design-software or coding experience.

## Product Purpose

From Sketchbook to Screen is a short, practice-led course that helps traditional artists understand web and graphic design, then apply their existing visual skills to clear, usable screen-based work. Success means a learner can make and explain intentional decisions about composition, whitespace, typography, colour, hierarchy, responsiveness, and interaction—and complete a small portfolio-ready project.

## Positioning

The course translates skills artists already possess—such as value, focal points, negative space, rhythm, colour relationships, texture, and close observation—into the constraints and opportunities of screen design, rather than treating design as a rejection of their existing practice.

## Operating Context

Learners work through ten brief lessons at their own pace, typically spending 15–25 minutes per lesson. Each lesson connects one screen-design concept to a familiar artistic skill, includes a small browser exercise, and invites application to a modest real subject such as an event poster, art portfolio, character commission page, or small shop page. A sketchbook or loose paper and a familiar drawing tool are useful alongside a browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The course covers whitespace, hierarchy, grids and alignment, typography, colour and contrast, balance and grouping, responsive composition, interaction, graphic systems, and a capstone critique loop.
- Lessons must remain brief, visual, and practice-led.
- The course assumes traditional-art experience and no prior design-software or coding background.
- Advanced front-end engineering, branding strategy, and professional print production are out of scope.
- The product is delivered as a static web course with browser-based interactive exercises.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named “From Sketchbook to Screen.” Its voice should feel like a modern art museum: thoughtful, composed, clear, culturally literate, and inviting without being overly casual or instructional in tone.

## Product Principles

1. Start from the learner’s existing artistic fluency and extend it to screens.
2. Teach usable visual judgment through small acts of making, not abstract vocabulary alone.
3. Keep the learning path approachable for people without technical training.
4. Connect visual expression to the practical needs of readers, viewers, and changing screen sizes.
5. End with a concrete, explainable piece of work the learner can be proud to show.
