20 minutes · Colour

Colour must still carry the message.

Your colour sensitivity is an asset. On a screen, it must share the job with legibility and clear states.

Skill: build a small palette that remains readable

Translate what you already know.

From paintingYou control temperature, saturation, and value to create depth, mood, and a focal point.
On a screenValue contrast is the non-negotiable layer beneath mood. Text, icons, and controls still need to be perceived in glare, fatigue, and varied vision.

One idea

Start a palette in value, then add hue. Use one strong accent for action or emphasis, not every label. Do not use colour alone to communicate an error, selection, or status; pair it with text, shape, or an icon.

For normal-size text, WCAG’s minimum contrast target is 4.5:1; large text has a 3:1 target. Read the primary source.

Retrieval check

Which property should you judge first when body text sits on a coloured background?

Make a five-swatch palette.

  1. Choose paper/background, ink/text, muted surface, accent, and optional second accent.
  2. Convert your sketch to grayscale or squint hard: can you still find text and the main action?
  3. Reserve the strongest accent for one recurring purpose.
  4. Check body-text pairs with a contrast checker before calling the palette finished.

Ask your teacher for a contrast or palette review. Share the hex values or a screenshot, not just a verbal description.
